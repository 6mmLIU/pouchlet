Privacy Policy

Pouch for Mac · Last updated November 4, 2026

Pouch does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What Pouch does on your Mac

  • Pouch reads and writes only the files and folders you give it — by dropping them on the app, opening them with Pouch, choosing them in a panel, or using Pouch from Finder, Quick Look, Shortcuts or AppleScript.
  • When you allow Pouch to use a folder, it remembers that permission on your Mac so it doesn’t ask again. You can remove it any time in Settings › General › Folder Access.
  • Passwords you choose to remember are stored in your own macOS Keychain. Pouch never sends them anywhere. You can remove them in Settings › General › Passwords.
  • Settings, presets and temporary files stay on your Mac.

What Pouch does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or registration.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting.
  • No network connections. Your archives and their contents never leave your Mac.

Children

Pouch collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the support page.

隐私政策（简体中文）

Pouch 不收集、不存储、也不分享任何个人数据。

  • Pouch 只读写你交给它的文件和文件夹：拖到 App 上、用 Pouch 打开、在面板中选择，或通过“访达”、快速查看、“快捷指令”、AppleScript 使用。
  • 你允许 Pouch 使用某个文件夹后，这个授权只保存在你的 Mac 上，以免重复询问；可随时在“设置 › 通用 › 文件夹访问权限”中移除。
  • 你选择记住的密码保存在你自己的 macOS 钥匙串中，Pouch 不会把它们发送到任何地方；可在“设置 › 通用 › 密码”中删除。
  • 设置、预设和临时文件都只保存在你的 Mac 上。
  • 没有账户，没有统计分析、广告、追踪或崩溃上报，也不进行任何网络连接。你的压缩包及其内容永远不会离开你的 Mac。

如有变更，会在本页发布新版本并更新日期。
